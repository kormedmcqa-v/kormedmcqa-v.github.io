KorMedMCQA-V Leaderboard

Multimodal Benchmark for Evaluating VLMs on the Korean Medical Licensing Examination

KorMedMCQA-V evaluates over 50 VLMs on 1,534 questions with 2,043 associated images from the Korean Medical Licensing Examination (2012–2023), covering X-ray, CT, ECG, ultrasound, endoscopy, and more.
KorMedMCQA-Mixed combines text-only and vision questions from specific exam years for unified evaluation.
All models are evaluated under a zero-shot protocol; accuracy is the primary metric.
